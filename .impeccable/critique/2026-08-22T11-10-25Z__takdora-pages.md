## Design Specificity Verdict

Takdora is partially authored: its warm sand/orange palette, Filipino-market copy, and manual-payment/BIR boundaries establish trust. Yet the owner experience still reads as a generic Radix card stack, rather than a distinctive booking-to-invoice operating system.

The deterministic scan found zero violations in `takdora/pages` and `takdora/ui`. That is useful but limited: the scanner has weak coverage of Reflex/Python UI sources and cannot detect broken navigation, workflow hierarchy, or decision overload.

## Overall Impression

The product has a credible, calm foundation and unusually honest product copy. Its biggest opportunity is to make the owner workflow feel like one guided operation rather than a set of separate forms.

## What's Working

- Trustworthy product truth: synthetic-demo labels, manual payment status, and BIR boundaries do not overclaim.
- The public booking flow includes useful validation, timezone context, and a confirmation state.
- Shared tokens, text labels, skip links on public surfaces, and touch-target intent form a solid accessibility baseline.

## Priority Issues

1. **[P1] Owner navigation is not reliable navigation.** The shell links Bookings and Invoices to nonexistent anchors and omits Dashboard, Setup, Services, and Availability. Replace this with route-based navigation, active state, and the same IA in the mobile menu. Suggested command: `$impeccable layout`.
2. **[P1] Setup has no guided readiness model.** The dashboard and onboarding do not make the publish path obvious. Add a persistent 3–4 step readiness checklist with status, direct CTAs, and a publish/preview moment. Suggested command: `$impeccable onboard`.
3. **[P1] Invoice work does not convey its financial consequence.** The draft is one long stack and issued actions compete equally. Group inputs, show a live summary, create a deliberate pre-issue review state, and isolate voiding/delivery actions. Suggested command: `$impeccable shape`.
4. **[P2] Public booking adds friction and leaks implementation detail.** It displays service IDs, requires a separate availability fetch, and uses dead header anchors. Hide IDs, load slots on service/date choice, keep the selected appointment summary visible, and provide valid contact/help paths. Suggested command: `$impeccable distill`.
5. **[P2] Availability overloads one page.** Weekly blocks, rules, closures, dragging, and preview compete at once. Use named days, staged cards, and progressive disclosure; drag-and-drop should be an enhancement. Suggested command: `$impeccable clarify`.

## Persona Red Flags

- **Jordan, first-time owner:** cannot see a reliable publish path; terms such as tax label, booking horizon, and weekday numbers require interpretation.
- **Casey, mobile customer:** encounters a long booking form, an extra slot-loading action, dead header links, and no persistent appointment summary.
- **Alex, experienced owner:** has no direct map of operations, saved filtering, bulk work, or fast invoice actions.
- **Sam, accessibility-dependent:** custom scheduling drag interactions lack an evident keyboard alternative; feedback/error semantics are inconsistent.

## Minor Observations

- Give the owner shell the same skip link as public and marketing surfaces.
- Use persistent labels consistently instead of placeholders on major forms.
- Make the marketing promise tangible with genuine workflow/product visuals rather than more generic SaaS composition.

## Questions to Consider

- What if the primary owner screen were one Today → complete → invoice workspace?
- What must be impossible to miss before an invoice number is frozen?
- Can booking feel like choosing a time with a local business, not filling out SaaS fields?
