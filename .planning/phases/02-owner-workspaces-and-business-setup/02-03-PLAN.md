<threat_model>
## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| Browser to onboarding state events | Untrusted field values attempt to update business identity and publication state. |
| Authenticated owner to Business profile | Tenant guards resolve one business before profile reads or writes. |
| Public slug to future public route | A human-readable URL identifies a business and may be changed over time. |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-02-09 | Tampering | profile and publish events | high | mitigate | Validate all profile fields server-side, authorize the owner through tenancy helpers, and centralize publication readiness checks. |
| T-02-10 | Spoofing | public slug | medium | mitigate | Normalize, reserve, and uniquely constrain canonical and redirected slugs; update slug and redirect record transactionally. |
| T-02-11 | Information disclosure | setup loader | high | mitigate | Load profile state only after owner/business authorization and use cross-tenant regression tests. |
| T-02-12 | Repudiation | public URL change | medium | mitigate | Retain immutable redirect records with timestamps and source slug after a published canonical address changes. |
| T-02-SC | Tampering | dependency installation | high | mitigate | Use installed Reflex, SQLModel, and shared UI components; add no unaudited package. |
</threat_model>
