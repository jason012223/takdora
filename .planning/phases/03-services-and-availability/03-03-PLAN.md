| Scheduling persistence to slot engine | Time-zone, rules, closures, and occupied intervals determine bookable instants. |

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |

| T-03-09 | Information disclosure | booked preview periods | high | mitigate | Define a neutral occupied-interval protocol and test that no customer fields reach the rendered projection. |
| T-03-10 | Denial of service | broad week/range calculations | medium | mitigate | Limit calculations to requested bounded weeks and persisted rule ranges; use injected time and indexed tenant queries. |
| T-03-11 | Tampering | timezone conversion | high | mitigate | Store policy with IANA zone, return aware UTC instants, and cover closure and DST boundaries with deterministic tests. |

</threat_model>
