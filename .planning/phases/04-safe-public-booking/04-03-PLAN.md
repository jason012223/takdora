<threat_model>
## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| Committed booking to outbox | Booking data crosses from database transaction to later mail dispatch. |
| Outbox to SMTP provider | Customer contact and appointment summary leave Takdora through configured credentials. |
| Booking result to anonymous page | Server outcomes control what a customer is told after submission. |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-04-09 | Repudiation | confirmation delivery | high | mitigate | Persist one booking-linked outbox record and state transitions before and after mail dispatch. |
| T-04-10 | Information disclosure | confirmation renderer | high | mitigate | Render from an explicit customer-safe snapshot containing only appointment and business contact data. |
| T-04-11 | Denial of service | repeat delivery dispatch | medium | mitigate | Make sent records idempotent, bound attempts, and keep mail work outside the booking transaction. |
| T-04-12 | Spoofing | public outcome copy | medium | mitigate | Use server-produced command and delivery states; do not allow page state or client text to claim a booking or email succeeded. |
| T-04-SC | Tampering | dependency installation | high | mitigate | Extend the existing SMTP boundary and Reflex stack; no package is installed. |
</threat_model>
