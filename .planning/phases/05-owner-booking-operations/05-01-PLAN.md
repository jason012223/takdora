<threat_model>
## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| Owner browser to booking query state | Date, view, status, and booking identifiers are untrusted inputs. |
| Authenticated owner to workspace bookings | A private booking record must be authorized by its business before projection. |
| UTC booking record to owner display | Stored appointment times are rendered in a business-local view. |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-05-01 | Information disclosure | owner booking lookup | high | mitigate | Derive the workspace from the authenticated owner and filter by business before every record lookup and list projection. |
| T-05-02 | Tampering | filter and view state | medium | mitigate | Normalize date, status, and view inputs server-side to bounded allowlists before constructing a query. |
| T-05-03 | Information disclosure | calendar projection | medium | mitigate | Keep calendar entries summary-only; reserve customer contact and notes for an authorized detail projection. |
| T-05-SC | Tampering | dependency installation | high | mitigate | Reuse the locked Reflex, SQLModel, and existing UI primitives; no package installation is planned. |
</threat_model>
