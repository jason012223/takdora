<threat_model>
## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| Owner browser to draft commands | Booking/invoice IDs and all editable customer and monetary values are untrusted. |
| Authenticated owner to workspace billing records | A private booking or invoice must be filtered by the derived active business. |
| Draft values to money calculation | Browser text becomes durable Decimal financial values. |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-06-01 | Information disclosure | draft lookup | high | mitigate | Derive the business from the authenticated owner and filter Booking and Invoice by business before every read or mutation. |
| T-06-02 | Tampering | draft creation and edits | high | mitigate | Require completed booking state, validate every edited field server-side, and persist draft data only in a transaction. |
| T-06-03 | Tampering | monetary calculations | high | mitigate | Use Decimal values and explicit decimal columns; normalize and reject invalid monetary input before totals are stored. |
| T-06-SC | Tampering | dependency installation | high | mitigate | This plan installs no package; it reuses locked Reflex, SQLModel, Alembic, and Python Decimal. |
</threat_model>
