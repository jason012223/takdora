# Phase 6 Research: Invoice Lifecycle and Manual Payments

## Chosen implementation approach

- Use tenant-owned SQLModel invoice, immutable invoice-line snapshot, payment-state, and PDF-document records in PostgreSQL.
- Use `Decimal` and database decimal columns for every monetary value; never calculate invoice totals with floats.
- Use ReportLab (`reportlab>=4.4,<5.0`) to render the issued invoice from an immutable snapshot to an in-memory byte stream. Its canvas supports binary streams and explicit A4 pages, so it avoids browser-rendering or external process dependencies. Keep the generated bytes in a database-backed document record for Phase 6; Phase 7 will replace the backing store with private object storage without changing owner authorization.
- Create invoice email outbox records only after issue/payment commands commit. Reuse the existing mail boundary and make each email request idempotent.

## Constraints

- Owner authorization derives the sole workspace from the server-side account; owner events never select a business from browser input.
- A draft starts only from an owned, completed booking. Issuing locks the draft and business sequence, assigns one business-scoped number, snapshots all rendering inputs, produces PDF bytes, and commits the immutable issued record.
- Generated documents say they are configurable business invoices and state that Takdora does not provide BIR registration, electronic invoicing, sales reporting, or an automatic compliance determination.
- A private PDF download must re-authorize the invoice through the active owner's business before returning bytes. No public URL or customer link is introduced.
